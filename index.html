Serving with love since 1983

Started as part of the esteemed 83 Madras Cafe family, Dosa Point has been a culinary landmark at Netaji Nagar Market for over four decades. We stay true to ancestral recipes: zero preservatives, natural stone-ground batter fermented for 14 hours, pure cow ghee, and fragrant sambar simmered with roasted coriander and shallots.
